# Summary research paper

`Summary_Paper.pdf` is the Springer LNCS summary. `Summary_Paper.tex`, `llncs.cls`, `learning_curves.png` and `sample_segments.png` are the rebuild files. Compile twice with `pdflatex Summary_Paper.tex` in this folder.

The final reported scores are cross-subject 83.72% (macro-F1 0.88), cross-view onto camera 1 80.62% (0.79), onto camera 2 83.25% (0.81), and onto camera 3 88.97% (0.83). `learning_curves.png` and Figure 6 plot those scores against the base paper. `sample_segments.png` shows frames from the demo clip.
